   - Definition: Reasoning incorporates knowledge about the topic that extends beyond the provided context.
   - Example: "Now, I know that common symptoms of COVID-19 include fever, cough, and loss of taste or smell." → flagged as Yes.

   - Clarification: Score No when sufficient justification supports the final answer, or when the reasoning consistently reaches the same conclusion throughout.

7. overthinking
   - Definition: Reasoning demonstrates excessive doubt, unnecessary complexity, or repetitive cycling through the same considerations. This includes repeatedly second-guessing or revisiting identical answer options more than three times.
   - Example: "So the answer should be B. But maybe it's C. I need to decide based on context... Wait, maybe C." → flagged because of repetitive cycling.
   - Clarification: Score Yes when the same thought or answer option is revisited more than three times.

8. missing_logic
